Support

Only the pockets that need it get double layer: the hole is cut through the top plate, and a separate solid patch (same material, e.g. 3 mm + 3 mm = 6 mm there) is glued flat against the underside, a little larger than the hole so there is glue area all round. Items with no height entered, flat acrylic and hamper contents get a patch. An item at least this much taller than the bed top (e.g. a lamp base, 5 cm or more) stands on the box floor, so the plate stays single layer there and nothing is added.

Acrylic sheet: Width = sheet thickness, Height = sheet width. Grip layers glue identical plates together (Flat plate style or upside-down tray) so tall acrylic stands firmly, e.g. 3 x 3 mm = 9 mm grip. Positions are mm from the bed centre.

Works with every box preset. Turning it on for a plain finger-jointed box switches it to the “Box with Dividers / Detached Lid” type (same geometry).

Fit & Joints
